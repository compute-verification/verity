09:08Z lowrank [QUESTION] (toolsmith) Two axes of the lowrank brief are closed by the fixed lora decoder. (1) Factor precision: CWLORA1 stores factors at 16 bits minimum (bf16/f16; f32 only costs bytes), so int8/int4 factors with per-row scales, which would buy 2-4x the rank per byte, can't be scored. (2) DoRA: W' = m * (W + BA)/||W + BA||_row is a per-output-row rescale, full rank in general, so neither lora nor full can carry it under 1 MB; the only magnitude-like terms CWLORA1 can carry are the norm gains (per-input-channel scales shared by q/k/v, by gate/up, and by the head). Would a CWLORA2 with quantized factors (int8/int4 + f16 row scales) and an optional per-row magnitude vector be accepted as a decoder version bump? Until then lowrank screens bf16 vs f16 (f16 rescales A, B to unit max and moves the factor into the header's scale) and norm gains as the DoRA-like term.
