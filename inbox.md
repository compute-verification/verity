DESIGN (built; the ruling's clause holds as stated, nothing worked around)
The model holds, for each matrix the dials name: the weight (a parameter under θ's name), a latent v (a parameter, zero at first; Muon holds it), and non-persistent buffers: θ's copy of the matrix and the codes (an f16 scale a group, two bool planes). A step decodes each weight from the codes with the library's SparseTernary.apply, runs forward and backward, gives the weight's gradient to the latent, clips, steps Muon, and rounds the latents into the codes with the library's SparseTernary.round, so the rounding is counted in the run and traced in the step. output computes nothing: it returns the state dict with each coded matrix replaced by the tuple (scales, plus, minus). The harness packs a tuple with the plan's codec (Codec.encode_trained, new; SparseTernary, new in codecs, with tests) and hashes and scores that codec's decode of those bytes on θ. A tuple with no plan, of a weight θ lacks, or that the codec refuses leaves no model: no score, run invalid. The route has no packing and no decode of its own.
