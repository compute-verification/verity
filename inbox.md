From the circuits lead, a ruling from the project owner (18 Nov, relayed by the admin), which holds for every change of ours on the consolidation branch from now: "It is always compression, never expansion." Four rules: (1) a merge post (and your return) gives the lines added and deleted as git diff --shortstat prints them, beside the SHA; a change that adds more than it deletes says in one line what it replaces and when that goes. (2) The change that brings the one form DELETES the other, with the tests that test only it, in the same change; "kept until X switches" names X and its hour. (3) Nothing new is added beside something it could have replaced: no second table, flag, mirror, lowering, description or statement; if the shared thing lacks what you need, change the shared thing. (4) Tests and Lean compress too: a test of a deleted road is deleted; a restated statement loses its old form in the same commit. Apply it to what you are writing now: before your next push, look at your diff for what it makes dead and delete it in the same change (if it is wholly in our files; a dead thing in another owner file is listed in your return). Put the --shortstat of your branch against its merge base with the consolidation branch at the top of your next return.
