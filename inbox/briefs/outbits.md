# OUTBITS: what a request's output is charged in the exfiltration capacity, and what would lower it on principle (reading and design; no code)

**Why.** The target of 300 GB a year over the world's datacenters is 1 bit in 7,400 requests. The lowest modeled figure the lane quotes (3,735 GB) assumes a request's output counts for half its bits, and nothing justifies that. Goal `proofs/spot-output-accounting`: "every output bit explained, certified or charged".

**Answer these, each with its place in the Lean or the code:**
1. **What is charged today.** In `Flock.Guarantees.SpotExfilTwo` the influence set has at most Σ 2^b(T) members. What is b(T) for a served request's output: which bits are counted as free, at an unsampled request, a sampled one, and one whose proof fails? Where does the cost model (`benchmarks/sampling_plan`, `optimize.py`) take the same quantity, and do the two agree?
2. **Which output bits the registered circuit already determines.** If the token select is in the registered circuit and its randomness is a committed value or the verifier's coins, the output tokens are a function of committed values, and an output bit is free only where a proof block is unsampled and wrong. Say exactly what is committed today (the sampler's seed, the temperature, the stop rule, the response's length), what the developer still chooses freely after seeing the weights, and how many bits a request that leaves.
3. **Options, smallest first,** each with: what it changes (a Definition, a committed value, a check), who owns it, what it does to the charge per request, and what it leaves free. Include: the sampler's randomness from the verifier's coins or a committed seed; lengths and stop rules capped and committed; the output charged only at unsampled requests (is that already so?); and whatever the Glossary's definition of exfiltration capacity (values, timing, lengths, routing) says is charged elsewhere.
4. **Your recommendation,** and what it would make the figures: the two-stage sampling design on the partition `main` defines (19,642 GB) and the best designed configuration (5,445 GB), both modeled at utilization 0.4. Arithmetic is enough; mark it as yours.
