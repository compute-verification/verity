# FIXEDCOST: what a second-stage session's fixed seconds are made of, and which part a second session of the same statement has to pay (read first, then measure)

**Why.** Daniel, Wed 11 Nov 8:19 PM PST: "the core thing is the tables in a session ... the core trade-off between exfiltration bound and binding error ... Are there ideas we haven't tried? ... I still want: >100 bits of security, <300 GB exfiltration bound at 10% compute cost."

The cost model reaches about 300 GB at a datacenter's 600 s only with sessions of up to 200 tables (296 to 316 GB at the best designed configuration, against 4,900 to 5,300 GB at one table a session; modeled). It gets there for one reason: it charges a statement's fixed seconds once a session whatever the session holds. That is an assumption nobody has measured (PATH300, Wed 21:51Z, `inbox/proofs-coordinator.md` on `cursor/verity-proofs-84b5`, about line 65706: "near 225 to 250 GB if such a session pays a statement's fixed seconds once [guess], nothing if it pays them for each statement. Not measured: no command proves more than 2"). And larger sessions cost a bit of binding error a doubling.

**The idea to test.** If most of the fixed seconds are paid once a statement and a process (reading the circuit, setting the prover up), then sessions of ONE table get the same saving from a prover's stream and a verifier that keep the statement loaded, at no cost in binding error. What is known:
- HEADTIME (same file, entries of Wed 21:44Z, 22:10Z and Thu 02:54Z, about lines 65386, 65972, 68297): the Lean verifier on the head block's statement is 159.9 s a session of which 127.6 s is reading the statement once a call; "one block proved on the CPU checks in the seconds of the whole statement ... the check's seconds follow the circuit, 77 to 161 ns a matrix entry, not the blocks drawn"; its kit and kept sessions are under the notes' `lanes/proofs/20261111T2118Z-headtime/` and in the store (`r20261112-022350-52e3`, `r20261112-023842-d85d`).
- The vLLM lane's #9090 (`flock-circuit prove --zk --stream`, head `7e57f859cf`, open): one prover process, one Lean session loop a session, "the statement's name and the Hello flags are the process's for every session".
- The limits (WIRES, Wed 22:19Z, about line 66484): one table a session on the GPU prover, 64 in the CPU prover and the Lean verifier, 127 at the challenger's coin commitment, 200 in the zero-knowledge scope.
- AGENTS.md, "Verifier cost: O(circuit description), never O(unrolled circuit)" (Daniel, 18 Oct).
