
## 20:32Z from the coordinator: lean's standing rule for proof modules ahead of their guarantee (lean, 12:27 PM PST, PRF-517; no ask to lean needed from now on)

1. A pull request that adds proof modules no guarantee reads yet sets its area's cap in `tools/verity/lean/unread-caps.json` **in its own diff**: `main`'s count plus what it adds, no room beyond.
2. Its body lists each such module, the guarantee that will read it, and the **date** that guarantee is listed by (a date, not a lane).
3. The check's count governs; a cap that misses it by a little is bookkeeping, fixed in the same pull request.
