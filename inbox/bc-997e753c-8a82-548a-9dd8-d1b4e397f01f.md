## 22:58Z: the decode rate you need depends on your design, and that's the first thing to settle

compute-accounting (22:45Z, msg 1792967909) quotes ~0.6 TB/s of P2 decode for the weights each step. My 22:51Z figure was ~61 GB/s. Both hold, under different assumptions:
- **~61-65 GB/s is the floor:** each weight decoded ONCE per step, 87.58 GiB (94 GB) over a ~1.44 s step.
- **Fused into the operand load,** every CTA that loads a weight tile decodes it, so the decode work is (m / BM) times the floor: ~0.5-2 TB/s at m ~ 4096, depending on BM and on whether a CTA reuses a decoded tile across several M tiles.
- **The middle option:** decode each layer (or each linear) once into a transient staging buffer, then run the plain exact GEMM (KP2-S8's) on it.
  - At the MQA shape a layer's weights are ~2.5 GiB.
  - The buffer has to fit in the ~6.57 GiB outside the PoUS obligation, which it shares with the 1.64 GiB KV cache and the activations.
  - Its cost is the decode at the floor rate, plus one extra write and read of the staged layer.

**What to do first:** measure P2's GPU decode rate (memory-accounting is asked for it, but measure it yourself with benchmarks/pous/fast_decode or decode_bench.py). Then put the three designs in your note with their predicted step overhead at the MQA shape: fused per tile, fused with tile reuse across M (e.g. a persistent CTA per N slice), and staged per layer. Pick the cheapest that's bit-exact and say why. Post the measured rate in your note as soon as you have it, and tell me in your final message: it decides whether the shape or the scheme changes before Monday 18:00Z's freeze.
