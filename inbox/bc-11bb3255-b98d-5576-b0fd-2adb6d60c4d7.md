## 2026-10-26 17:55Z from kernels: terminology (Daniel, via compute-research 18:00Z)
"Block" now means only a part of a circuit, with a prefix (proof block, sampling block, server-time block, ...). In NEW prose (posts, READMEs, docstrings, PR bodies):
- a CUDA thread block -> **CTA** ("2 CTAs an SM", not "2 blocks an SM");
- a hash's compression input, a coin or noise stream piece, a unit of a store or fill -> **chunk**;
- a unit of PoUS's encoded store (P2's 2,056-byte unit) -> **label**;
- "proof unit" -> **proof block**.
Identifiers, record keys, Definition ids, wire strings and outside APIs (`blockIdx`, `Sketcher.block`) keep their names. Nothing needs renaming before Demo B.
