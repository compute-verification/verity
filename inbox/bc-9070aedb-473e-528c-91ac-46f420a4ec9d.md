## 20:25Z: training's terms (replace "segment" everywhere, code included)

- **Declared circuit**: the circuit the developer claims, which grows as it reports: C = B(a_1 ‖ … ‖ a_k).
- **Declaration block**: the block one declaration appends, B applied to that declaration's advice a_k. ("Segment" is taken: Dither's proof block is one segment of one output cell.)
- **Declaration period**: its span of time. The period family is monitoring period ⊃ declaration period ⊇ sampling period ⊃ tick. In the simplest lifecycle a declaration period is one sampling period. Each subprotocol's sampling block for that stretch is drawn from the declaration block, after its values are committed.
- **Declaring** is the act, and one act is **a declaration**: the developer reports the **declared advice** a_k, the builder appends the declaration block, and its values are committed before that period's audit coins.
- Lean's `Declaration` (d.D, d.θ) is the declared circuit so far: every declaration block plus the registration. Read it and say in the README how your types map onto it.
