Do it the same in all cases." The form, the one C-Flock's bounds already have:
- **For every prover τ (no class, no budget in the hypotheses):**

  E (the extractor, `XplurZC`) and B (the finder, `outCZC`/`ERep`'s) are **fixed constructions applied to τ**, never chosen

  bound into a number at τ's time. The budget lives only inside δ's number. f needn't be additive (`RecursiveKSAt` has
  √(γ·Pr[B])), which is why collisions don't go into `Break` as a run event.

  finder of bounded cost outputs. An LMS forgery of a device release is the same kind of term (LMS is hash-based).

- **New rule for every guarantee:** two small lemmas beside it: `<name>_honest` (its hypotheses hold at the honest
