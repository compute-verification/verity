### 1. What `Spot.HistoryTable.checkAt` checks and costs

- **What it checks (read).** `checkAt` (`Spot/HistoryTable.lean:167-176` on `checked`) refuses, in order:
  - a capacity that isn't a power of two of at least 2;
  - a row of no words;
  - more rows than the capacity;
  - a `b ‖ c` string that isn't 128 bytes;
  - a registered root that isn't 64 bytes;
  - a registered root other than `rootAt cap (domainAt cap program table words) bcs (placeholder words)`.
- **What it reads (read).** Only the written rows' 128-byte `b ‖ c` strings (each row's hiding commitment), in position order. It never reads the rows, the salts or the writers' roots. Rank r's leaf binds r (`leafAt`, `:83-84`). From `bcs.size` up, every leaf is the placeholder's: the zero row under the zero salt.
- **What it costs.** It is per table, per registration that declares it: 2^17 leaves and 2^17 − 1 nodes, whatever the table's length and however many reads. Reads cost the verifier nothing here, because they are proved inside their own units. At #8357's measured 1.90 µs per 64-byte message and 3.39 µs per node, a 128k request's 78 tables take 98 core-seconds (mine).
- **Round 2 can call it as it is.** It is a pure function of `(cap, program, table, words, bcs, registered)` (read). What is one-stage is around it:
  - The domain binds the registration's program digest under the tag `verity/one-stage/history-table/v1` (`:46`, `:63-71`).
  - On #8416, the strings come from the writer members' public bodies under the registration's leaf layer, with positions mapped to one writer template's units. See `order` (`:143-162`), and #8416's `memberStrings`, `tableStrings` and `tableRoots` (`HistoryTable.lean:210`, `:233`, `:276`).
- **What changes under spans (mine).** The writers are units inside span interiors, which have public bodies only when drawn, so round 2 can't recompute the root from its writers. Two ways:
  - **(A)** Serve publishes the table's strings: 16.8 MB a table, 1.3 GB for a 128k request's 78. Hiding commitments may be public. Round 1's registration runs `checkAt` over them as held runs (`From.held`, `:97-103`).
  - **(B)** Serve registers only the root. Round 2 opens each drawn span's run of leaves against it.
  - (A) also pins the padding to the placeholder. (B) leaves the padding unchecked, which is harmless only while no read can reach it (item 3).

### 4. The candidate form

- **Not as a boundary port, for three reasons (read):**
  - **Shape.** A record port's tree is `window_domain(program, partition, port, schema, leaves, run)` over exactly its own leaf count (`verity/ml/commitments/window.py:24-26`).   - **Links.** `rowsOk` (`SecondStage.lean:563-574`) gives replay block b the rows `[b·r, (b+1)·r)`, with `r = leaves / units`. So every span would have to own the same number of rows of every table, which prefill slices beside decode steps don't.

- **No range check is needed in this form (mine).** A read at a position in `[start, p]` opens the same table, whose rows there are the span's own, held to it by item 5.
- **What the read needs instead:**
  - its key wired from the index (circuits' and vllm's: #8401, #8391);

That is a new pair of statics on `EmbeddingRead_v1` or a new Definition, and it would be circuits' change. The merge would also have to split the span's own keys from carried ones.
