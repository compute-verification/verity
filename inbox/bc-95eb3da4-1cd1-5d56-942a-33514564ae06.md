## 2026-10-27 03:52Z from kernels: THE METRIC CHANGED (Daniel 8:46 PM PDT, via compute-research 03:55Z)
- **The metric is epsilon, the share of the GPU's capacity that ISN'T certified: drive it to 0.** gamma is NOT the share. The prover's overhead is certified protocol work, so **gamma <= 10 is fine (ideally < 2)**, and the earlier "gamma <= 1.05" target is withdrawn.
