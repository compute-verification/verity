## 2026-11-05T07:08Z from proofs (SAMPLEFIRST: PRF-409 answered, comment 0e30c73d)
- **Verdict:** the tree is right, and commit-before-coin is the real distinction, but r rounds of sampling is not the design to build. Against an adaptive prover, sampling k of n children inside a drawn block costs $\frac{\Lambda}{\varepsilon}[\frac{n}{k}(\rho+c)+p]$, so k = n wins at every depth. Clustered draws with everything committed (variant 2) gain nothing for additive harm, because the worst case fills whole clusters.
- **The family:** one-stage sampling over a cut of the tree, called **audit blocks**. Their boundaries are committed at serve time. A drawn one is proved in full, as one proof block or as several over a **deferred interior** committed after the draw. Today's design is the case where audit blocks are the proof blocks. Its parameters are the cut, the one-stage design over it, and a harm weight on audit blocks.
- **KS:** SPOT's shape with $\bar P_a(B)=\mathbb E_S\prod_{v\in B\cap S}e_v$ ($e_v = 0$ when v is fully proved, so it reduces to today's $P_a$ over the cut). An undrawn faulty audit block is charged whole.
- **Lean:** SPOT doesn't map `Arg` to `Arg`, because an inner audit isn't knowledge-sound for its parent. Don't build SPOT over SPOT. Build one argument-composition lemma (commit the interior, prove every block) plus a `Refines` lemma that correct children make a correct parent.
- **Lean, the extraction contract:** `Sessions.interior` is used only in `Assemble.lean`'s split. An existential `opens` (the witness hands back its interior) should drop it and fit `SamplingGeneric` as is. Try that first. `Claims.Recursion` is unchanged.
- **Don't build:** variant 1 with k < n, variant 2, or `TwoStageLaw` as a law. Keep `Audit/TwoStage.lean` as the proof that inner sampling doesn't pay.
- **Numbers** (served-window model, ε 1% at 2^-40): coarse audit blocks cost 3–8% of serving pooled over a datacenter week, month or year, against 1.6–2.1× today. Break-even is 1.2–1.5 machine-days for a layer of a token, 1.5–1.9 datacenter-days for whole short requests, and about a datacenter-year for whole long requests. Per window they lose by 300× or more.
- **Names:** "audit block", "deferred interior", "replay cost" (a node's cost, not a level). Retire "replay block", "replay unit" and `ReplayDomain`-as-level. Avoid "two-stage" in code: there is one draw and two commitment times.
- **Smells with fixes are in the comment's §3:** three Partition types, harm weighted per proof block, "everything committed at serve time" stated as a protocol property, replay "from the last committed state", "h" meaning two things, and weights opened per block (69–88% of the AND gates).
