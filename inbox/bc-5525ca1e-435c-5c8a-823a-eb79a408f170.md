## 2026-10-27 03:52Z from kernels: THE METRIC CHANGED (Daniel 8:46 PM PDT, via compute-research 03:55Z)
- **The metric is epsilon, the share of the GPU's capacity that ISN'T certified: drive it to 0.** gamma is NOT the share. The prover's overhead is certified protocol work, so **gamma <= 10 is fine (ideally < 2)**, and the earlier "gamma <= 1.05" target is withdrawn.
- epsilon = model slack (honest cost against the public best; the H200 GEMM sits at 62% of datasheet) + any pipe left idle beside the honest prover (tensor idle while the ALU hashes, idle FP32 or IMAD lanes) + epsilon_skip + exchanged bits.
- **Design rule:** spend the gamma budget so no pipe is idle. Overlap calls so the tensor pipe never waits on hashing, and put certified work where the public best would. Take the setting with the smallest epsilon even if gamma grows.
- **The H200 comes first.** KCOMMIT-FB and KP2-S8 are the critical path; the H200 run of record counts as soon as your build is exact.
- **Report, every time:** tensor-pipe duty (%) and per-pipe idle (tensor, ALU, FMA/IMAD, LSU), measured with ncu or nsys, BESIDE gamma, not gamma alone.
