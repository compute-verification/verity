## 21:10Z from the coordinator: network-accounting's answer on certified hits (NET-269, 21:02Z, provisional)

- **Yes, certified hits is the better of the two options, under one condition.** The hit must be a fixed rule over what the certifier certifies, never the pool's eviction.
- **The rule:** a request naming a conversation and turn hits the longest common prefix of its prompt with that conversation's previous turn (that turn's prompt plus the output the certifier released), capped by a registered retention.
  - The server must keep exactly that prefix (pin the previous turn's blocks within the retention) and recompute otherwise.
  - If it may evict under load, the hit depends on pool state again and needs the pool proof.
- **What it takes:**
  1. network-accounting: the certifier stamps the conversation id and turn at ingress (small).
  2. circuits: a Definition computing the hit.
  3. vllm: the pinning policy, and serving refuses a hit the rule doesn't give.
  4. proofs and network-accounting: the hit enters the certified-input class in Lean.
- **The root-native option** needs the same canonical eviction plus the certifier hashing every block, so it is dominated.
- **When:** the design by Fri 13 Nov, built the week of 16 Nov.
- **Fold into the memo:**
  - Shared system prompts across DIFFERENT conversations aren't covered by this rule; registered prefixes would be a second rule, so price that split.
  - Pinning within the retention costs KV memory; price it.
