C. **Where the wires will not pass as things stand.** A wire reads one writer's row whole. A port read from several writers is cut k ways along its outer elements, or read through an `AllGather_v1` whose parts are cut (`interface.py:38-45`). The limits are at most 256 rows, one row past 111 B, and 2^27 bits (`interface.py:307-311`, `322-344`).
   - The collectives (all-reduces, the fused norm, the logits' all-gather): both forms are used, and they pass at one and two prompt tokens (`test_tp_steps.py:83-108`, read, not run). Per rank, coordinate words go to 96-word rows through `AllGather_v1{WORLD=96, N=1}` (`grain.py:5-7`; `chunk(6144)` is 96). Each chunk's combine reads the W ranks' 96-word rows through its parts cut 8 ways, and `AllGather_v1{WORLD=64, N=96}` builds the 6,144-word row. Every unit there is under 256 rows. Inferred: at the class in part A, `AllReduceMultimem{SIZE=P·6144}` is the same chunking at more chunks, so it fits.
   - Whole-row readers after a COORDS or COLS split: the q/kv projections, then RoPE and the norms; MoeSum, then the residual add. A 6,144-word row from one-word coordinate writers is 6,144 writer rows, past 256 for the cut form. It needs the same two-level gather that `tp/grain` builds (96, then 64), which fits. Today no code emits it outside a collective site (part B).
   - Attention's and the DSA indexer's reads of earlier positions: one writer row per key, so a port cut once per key holds up to P + O − 1 rows. At (256, 32) that is 287, past 256, so key-block chains (CHAINS, 64 keys a block) or a gather are needed; at the class in part A it is @@KEYS@@.
   - Where neither form fits, inferred from the node's templates: a key's latent row is written by two Definitions, `DsaRMSNorm` (512 words) and `RoPEHeadGptJ` (64), so one outer element of the reader's port comes from two writers of unequal width. The cut form splits only between outer elements, and `AllGather_v1` takes equal parts. The registered rank form has one writer here, `DsaLatentRow` (22,386 calls, run …e9ba, part 1), so building from the registered form rather than the node form removes it. The same holds anywhere a reader takes part of a writer's row, which `wires.py:34-35` leaves as a free input (`Sites.unwired`). How many such reads there are is not established until a lowering's `settle` names them.
