SPOT turns any proof system that is knowledge sound for single proof blocks into one for a whole partitioned computation, proved once for every such system, with Flock as the deployed instance. On main: knowledge soundness with every extractor's work counted, the cost bounds, zero knowledge and the Flock corollary. What sets the date is its last piece, [the two-stage guarantee proved at the running code](#proofs/spot-two-stage-sound), which three @proofs workers hold.
