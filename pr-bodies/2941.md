  - **Proofs' verifier of record ([#2946](https://github.com/compute-verification/verity/pull/2946), CHAL1, PRF-117), merged in.** `trusted_rounds_reason(commits, directory, ledger, window, key)` is #2946's: `remote.trusted_session` (the auditor's signed v1 ledger opens and ends the window's one session, and the challenger's signed close signs its release log), then the window's coins against that commitment, the salt after the weights' root, the rounds through PoUW's round rule of record (`core_check.rounds`, Lean `PouwCheck.roundsCheck`), and then H1's draw check (RT4-INT-19).
  - **The ledger is checked once.** `challenger.audited_session` reads only a v1 ledger (canonical lines, opens and ends, `window` = `{commit, period}`) and a signed `challenger.json` close. Infra's live ledger is v1 entries then v2 (reserves, two-step opens, periods), and its sessions' `challenger.json` is infra's unsigned summary, so #2946 checks nothing of it. The v2 layer stays here under `TODO(#2946 v2 follow-up, cursor/challenger-ledger-v2-7c3e)`: the `Ledger` mirror of infra's `verify_ledger`/`verify_periods` (the period kinds, the two-step reserve and open, `period_reason`), `evidence` and `released_v2` (infra's JSON-lines log, float times; the end entry's seal stands in for a signed close). `session_reason` then checks the coins exactly as `trusted_rounds_reason` does, with the round rule `core_check.rounds` applies (`window.rounds_reason` and the log's roots in order). A v1 entry's signed message is `challenger.ledger_message`, reused rather than mirrored. Once the follow-up lands, the mirror goes and this module keeps only vllm's own checks (one session per window, the commitment before the first coin, the slot tables).
  - The pouw setting `session` (and the Commit's `--replay-session`) is the gate's answer to `reserve --peer NAME --scheme S --families JSON`, then `open --session S --root R`: `session`, `seq`, `root` (the window's root) and `commitment` (the session's coin commitment root; from `status SESSION` when the answer's is null), plus optionally `host`, `port`, `peer`, `by`, `for`, `reserve`, `opened_by` and `secret`. The window's commitment and the Commit's record carry it without the `secret` (`carried`).
  - Serving side (`pouw_h1_challenger.connect`): the hello carries the answer's `secret`, and a challenger whose `commitment.root` isn't the answer's `commitment` is closed and refused before its salt coin (or the replay coin).
  - `Ledger.verify` mirrors infra's `verify_ledger` over one published head, under the verifier's own key (`pinned_key`, pin from config). It checks v1 entries then v2 in one chain (no v1 after a v2), `seq` from 0, the `prev` SHA-512 chain, each `sig` under `entry_message` with the schema's tag, LMS leaves strictly increasing, one session a root and one root a session, a v2 end's `record_leaf` between its session's open and end leaves, used once and no entry's leaf, each two-step open bound to its session's `reserve` (its seq), carrying the reserve's request (`by`, `for`, `peer`, `scheme`, `epoch`, `families`, `minutes`) unchanged, opened by its prover (`opened_by` in {`by`, `for`}) inside the reserve's minutes and before its period's close, no one-step v2 open after the first reserve, each reserve first in its session with `expires_utc` = start + minutes, and (beyond `verify_ledger`) the `key_sha512` of every reserve, open and period-open equal to the verifier's key.
  - **Periods (verify_periods-style).** `Ledger.verify` also mirrors `verify_periods`. A period-open or period-close has a null session and is v2. A period's name is used once, and a lane has one open period at a time. A period-open's `cap` and `circuit` are optional, but when either is present the cap is a positive integer and the circuit a lowercase SHA-512. A period's windows are exactly its lane's reserves and one-step v2 opens (prover `for`, else `by`) between its period-open and period-close, at most `cap`. A close must list exactly those windows, in order, as `{root, session, complete}` (root null for a reserve never opened), with `complete` true only when it lists at least one window and all are complete. `Ledger.periods` returns them.
  - `Ledger.newest` takes every published `challenger-ledger/v2` head, verifies each one, refuses two that don't extend one another, and returns the longest, so the newest label alone is never trusted.
  - The verifier reads the record only from the ledger and the gate's `fetch SESSION` tree, never from the prover. `session_reason` refuses:
    - a window with no session, or another Commit;
    - a root the ledger has no session for;
    - a session other than the one the ledger opened for the root;
    - an answer `seq` other than the open entry's;
    - a session that never ended, ended with `closed` false, or ended without `complete`;
    - an end entry whose `commitment` isn't the window's;
    - any file of `out/` other than the one the end entry's `files` sealed (another session's tree, a rewritten `releases.jsonl`, a missing tree);
    - a `key.json` that isn't the key the open pins;
    - a `record.json` signed for another `host` than the open's `vy-challenger-1.<session>`, for another scheme, epoch or families, under another commitment than the end's, or at another leaf than the end's `record_leaf`;
    - a release log whose per-family counts aren't the end's `released`;
    - weights other than the registered root;
    - and then the coins, checked as `trusted_rounds_reason` checks a v1 session's.
  - `period_reason(ledger, period, accepted)` takes a period name, finds its period-open and period-close in the verified ledger, and refuses:
    - a period the ledger doesn't have;
    - a missing close ("an open period credits nothing");
    - an accepted window that isn't one of the period's windows;
    - a close that isn't complete: no window, or a window reserved and never opened, never ended, unclosed or short of its coins (every window counts);
    - a window of it, ended complete, with no accepted window (abandoned after its coins).
    The old `prover`/`seqs`/`fetch` arguments are gone: membership comes from the signed period entries, and a complete window's end already says it released every coin.
  - Record fields bound:
    - reserve: `by`, `for`, `minutes`, `started_utc`, `expires_utc`, `key_sha512`, and the request fields an open must carry;
    - open: `window.root` (v1: `window.commit`), `key_sha512`, `host`, `scheme`, `epoch`, `families`, `by`/`for` (the period's lane), `reserve`, `opened_by`, `started_utc` (two-step), and the entry's `session` and `seq`;
    - period-open/period-close: `period`, `lane`, `cap`, `circuit`, `key_sha512`, `windows`, `complete`;
    - end: `closed`, `complete`, `commitment`, `record_leaf`, `released`, `files`;
    - every entry: `schema`, `seq`, `prev`, `sig` and its leaf.
    Not bound: `pinned`, `port`, `secret_sha512`, `rc`, `ended`, `refused_hellos`, `opened_utc`, `closed_utc`, `ended_utc` and `request.json`. A period's `circuit` is recorded but not compared to the window's circuit here (CHAL1's check).
  - Tests (`test_rt4_h1_challenger.py`): `…held_to_the_one_session_the_auditors_ledger_opened_for_its_commits_root`, `…ledger_refuses_two_sessions_for_one_root…`, `…period_is_its_lanes_windows_between_its_signed_open_and_close…`, `…ledger_refuses_periods_and_reserves_off_the_gates_rules`, `…session_setting_is_the_gates_open_answer…`, `…challenger_off_the_answers_commitment_is_refused_before_its_first_coin`, `…record_is_tied_to_its_session…` and `…infras_v2_head_and_sessions_bind_under_the_gates_key`. The last runs on infra's own bytes: the live head art:fbe4bcb48b (31 entries, periods infra-smoke-20261020a to d) and seven sessions as the gate's `fetch` gave them, a registered fixture/v1 (art:67f611682f, `tests/protocol_options/fixtures/challenger_periods`, replacing art:1054b304bd).
