## R9 — coverage of every selected equation

**Status: EA (verifier-side gap) in `verifier_v2`; a conforming module is `vllm-poc/e2e/coverage_v1/` (this lane, see schema below).**

What the mainline verifier does (`verifier_v2/reverify.py`, stage V5, lines 322-377):

- The *expected* set is read from the prover's own `mapping.units.jsonl` (`:324-325`, `by_vu`); the verifier checks only that the set of VU indices equals the selection (`:329`, `V5.selection`). The *location* of each selected VU (request, t, layer, op, j) is the prover's assertion; nothing derives it from the partition + circuit.
- Coverage is counted from the prover's `composed.json` field `selected_vus` (`:343-357`): `got_coord[vu] += 1` on the prover's claim, cross-checked by `unit_matches` (`:106-116`) against the prover's `provenance.units` — two prover-supplied lists compared with each other. The bound typed object (`record["object"]`, the thing `check_binding` binds to the proof at `:308`) is read only for its *count* of obligations (`:358-361`, `V5.no_extra`) and its roots (`:362-365`); its obligations' `target` / boundary references are never compared to the selection.
- Consequence: a prover can prove coordinate (t′, j′) while labelling provenance/mapping with the selected (t, j) consistently; V5 passes. And the "omitted equation hidden behind duplicate coverage" case passes too: two obligations for VU A labelled A and B in provenance pass `unit_matches` only if the labels are consistent with mapping — which the prover writes. `dup` (`:371`) only detects a VU *labelled* twice.
- Batching: `check_binding` (`src/veritor/interface`, called `:308`) proves the typed object is what the proof checked, so the partition is not changed *inside* a proof; but since coverage is by label, batching can hide an omission (above).

Legal empty Q: V5 with `selected["count"] == 0` passes trivially (`:329`), and every stage after it iterates nothing — conforming by accident, untested.

The legacy record's V5 (REC-P1-B0, `vllm-poc/scripts/rec_p1_verify.py:292-325`, read at `b47e0d85`) — my own reading, agreeing with A2's M1: `prov_units = record["provenance"]["units"]` (`:296`) is zipped with the prover's `selected_vus.coordinates` (`:299`) and compared by `unit_matches(u, pu)` with the located unit `u` from `by_vu` — i.e. the *prover-written* provenance label is compared with the description-located unit; the object's own `boundary.in/out` references (the only thing the guest authenticates, `kernel/openings.rs`) are never read. `V5.coordinates` (`:319`) therefore certifies "every selected VU is *labelled* by exactly one obligation", and `n_ob == len(prov_units) + n_eq` (`:311-313`) counts obligations. "Expected statement" in the legacy record meant *matching prover metadata*, not the positions the fixed circuit and partition imply. **EA**, closed on the verifier side by A2's `veritor.interface.expected` (positions of one object) and this lane's `coverage_v1` (per-VU accounting over all proofs).

Fix (implemented here on top of A2's module, mainline `b47e0d85`, R9-conforming by construction):
- **Expectation derivation is A2's** — `veritor.interface.expected.gemm_coordinate_expectation` / `input_equation_expectation` produce an `ExpectedObligation` (ordered `ExpectedValue(tree, leaf, offset)` in/out, subcircuit digest, prescribed Input words) from the located unit (`rec_p1_obligations.ProgramLocator` + `Description`), never from `provenance`. `coverage_v1` does not re-derive positions; it consumes those objects.
- `coverage_v1.expected_statements(circuit, partition, Q) -> {vu_id: ExpectedStatement}`: `partition.locate(vu_id)` returns the A2 expectation; `circuit.kind_program(vu_id, expectation)` the `(family, subcircuit digest)`; the digest is `tagged_sha256("veritor/coverage_v1/statement", {family, subcircuit, incoming, outgoing, pins})`. Refuses (never skips) an unlocatable or repeated VU, a missing outgoing value, a subcircuit disagreement, two VUs with one statement.
- `coverage_v1.check_coverage(expected, proofs, *, trees=, rank_of=) -> CoverageReport`: obligations of every *accepted* proof are read from the **bound** typed object (`obligations_of_typed_object`: `leaf.root`, `leaf.id`, `reference.offset`, `obligation.subcircuit`, the `Input` gate's `value` per outgoing slot) and matched by digest → per VU covered (exactly one) / uncovered / duplicate, plus `unexpected` obligations. With `trees` (A2 `CommittedTree`) and `rank_of` (A2 `rank_oracle` over the committed id lists), A2's `check_expected_positions(obj, expectations-this-proof-matched, trees=, rank_of=)` runs per proof; any problem (root/count/framing, rank, layout, unused leaf/ref/root, pin) **voids that proof's coverage** — its VUs become uncovered.
- Tests `tests/contract/a56_test_coverage_v1.py` (13 pass at `b47e0d85`'s `expected.py`): duplicate-hides-omission, empty Q, batching across proofs, re-submitted proof, larger-unit / other-root / other-family obligation covers nothing, pinned word is part of the statement, refusals, position-check voiding, and the archived INT-B0 typed object (all 30 obligations (6 GemmCoordinate, 24 InputEq) located from their *declared* boundary and passing A2's check; a provenance-relabelled duplicate exposed).
