# C-Flock design ledger

This ledger lists every candidate C-Flock has had for its statement and row formats, its scopes and its proof routes, each
with its status, the reason in one sentence and the evidence. A retired candidate, and a headline that `flock_verify_sound`
superseded, keeps its record here with the commit that proved it; the derivations are in `verifier/lean/soundness/DESIGN.md`.
Experimental means under development and refused by the verifier of record; retired means refused and superseded, by the
canonical format or by `flock_verify_sound`.

**Cited**, the working reading (the definition is still with Daniel): covered by `flock_verify_sound` and accepted by the
verifier of record, or cited by Table 1, a claim (`verity.claims`) or a protocol's code, as
`protocols/sampled_proofs/verity_sampled_proofs/one_stage/draw.py` cites `work_escape_le`.

Commits are on `main` unless the row names a branch: fail-closed is `cursor/verifier-fail-closed-95d4`, where
`flock_verify_sound` lands, and canonical-io is `cursor/canonical-io-95d4`. Section numbers are `verifier/PROTOCOL.md`'s.
